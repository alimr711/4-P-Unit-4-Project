My Unofficial Local Guide to The Bronx

Lloyd's Carrot Cake

Located off the last stop on the 1 train going to the Bronx, Lloyd's Carrot Cake is the best thing to buy! Some things sold here are:

  • Their famous carrot cake with and without nuts. They are made fresh everyday, by the hour.
  • Chocolate cake. Personally I have never had this but this also sells out quickly.
  • Pineapple coconut cake. Made with coconut shavings, another phenomonal option.
There are many other flavors, but these are the most popular and highly recommended!



Grounds Donut House

Located in Throggs Neck, Grounds is home of many different flavored donuts. Over the years they have exoanded their variety to include:

  • Regular strawberry, vanilla, and chocolate donuts.
  • Fruity pebble donuts
  • Boston Creme donuts
  • Apple Pie donuts
  • Churro donuts
  • Coco Pebble donuts
  • Coffe cake donuts
  • Maple Bacon donuts
  • Cookies and Creme donuts
There are also many other flavors still being made. The donut house also sells drinks, and new suffed bagels. Both garlic flavored and cinnamon toast flavored which are worth a try!
